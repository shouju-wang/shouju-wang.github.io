I’m Shouju Wang, a Ph.D. student at the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa advised by Prof. Haopeng Zhang. I study how language-model agents can respect privacy, reason about social context, and act reliably in the real world.

Research

Making agents safer by design.

I build evaluation methods and practical safeguards for AI agents operating with sensitive information and social norms.

01

Privacy & contextual integrity

Understanding when an agent’s use of information is appropriate—not merely whether that information is private.

02

Reliable language-model agents

Evaluating and aligning LLM and VLM agents that perceive, reason about, and act across complex workflows.

03

Fair machine learning

Developing causal approaches to reduce bias while retaining useful performance in graph-based anomaly detection.

Hello World: Starting My Ph.D. Journey

March 2026 · 3 min read

It feels surreal to finally write this. After four years at Wuhan University and an incredible internship at Microsoft Research Asia, I have officially begun my Ph.D. at the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa.

Why privacy & LLM agents?

During my time at Microsoft Research Asia, I worked on understanding how LLM-powered agents handle privacy in realistic settings. The more I dug into the problem, the more I realized how fundamental the gap is between what these models know about privacy norms and how they actually behave when deployed as agents.

That privacy judgment–action gap became the seed of my Ph.D. research.

What I’m working on

My current research focuses on multimodal contextual integrity for LLM and VLM agents. Contextual integrity, originally proposed by Helen Nissenbaum, provides a framework for thinking about privacy as appropriate information flow. I am interested in extending this framework to multimodal settings where agents process images, text, and structured data at once.

Privacy is not about secrecy. It is about the appropriate flow of information in context.

Life in Hawai‘i

Moving from Wuhan to Honolulu has been quite a change. The campus is surrounded by mountains, the weather is beautiful, and the research environment has been warm and collaborative. I am grateful for the opportunity to work with Prof. Haopeng Zhang and the people in our lab.

This space is where I plan to share research thoughts, paper readings, and reflections on the Ph.D. journey. Thanks for reading.
